# Agent instructions

## Product invariant

> **Blast Radius may miss something it cannot prove; it must never present something it cannot prove as an Impact.**

Before designing or implementing product behavior, read `docs/product-contract.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, and relevant ADRs. Do not trade this invariant for broader apparent coverage.
